# Teaching Notes

## How the learner wants to be taught

- Explain _why_ things work, not just what they are. Go deep; don't oversimplify.
- One exercise per concept, rotating domains: software/distributed systems → fintech operations → aircraft maintenance.
- Predict before reveal: commit to how a stock behaves or which loop wins before seeing the answer.
- Check diagrams critically. Wrong polarities and missing delays are exactly the mistakes to catch.
- Don't advance until the current concept is demonstrated. Skip what's already shown.
- Name the trap when the learner falls into linear thinking during an exercise.
- Cite sources in lessons; flag where Meadows, Senge and Sterman disagree.
- Sessions 30–45 min. Mermaid or plain text diagrams.
