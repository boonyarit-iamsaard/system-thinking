They already know repeat-defect tracking, deferred-defect lists and the tension between hotfix and root cause from real practice. Use these as anchors rather than teaching them, and expect pattern-level thinking to come easily. Structure-level explanation and formal notation (stocks, flows, loop polarity) are where to probe.
